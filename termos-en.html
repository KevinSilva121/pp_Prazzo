1.What Prazzo is

A tool for merchants to record and follow instalment sales, store credit and payment books: inventory, customers, sales, instalments, receipts and profit.

What Prazzo is NOT — and this matters:

  • It is not a financial institution. We do not lend money, we are not a party to any sale of yours, and we do not intermediate payments between you and your customer.
  • It does not process your customers' payments. When you record that an instalment was paid, that is a note you made. No money passes through Prazzo.
  • It is not a credit reporting service. We do not query and do not feed any credit bureau. Marking a customer as overdue in Prazzo does not blacklist anyone.
  • It is not accounting, legal or financial advice. The figures the app calculates, profit included, are an aid — not an accounting record.

5.Free plan

Prazzo has a permanent free plan. It is limited in how many records you can create and it does not include some features, but it does not expire and does not turn into a demo.

6.Prazzo Pro, free trial and renewal

The paid plan removes the limits and unlocks additional features. The price, the period (monthly or yearly) and the conditions are shown in the app before purchase, always using the official amounts reported by your device's store (Google Play or the App Store) in your currency.

Free trial: new subscribers get 3 days on iPhone and 5 days on Android. The period that applies to you is always the one shown on the subscription screen, before you buy. Please read this carefully — at the end of the trial the subscription is charged automatically, at the amount and period you chose, unless you cancel first. Cancelling during the trial results in no charge at all.

Automatic renewal: the Prazzo Pro subscription renews automatically at the end of each period, and your device's store (Google Play or the App Store) charges the amount then in force, until you cancel.

Price changes: we may change prices. Any change will be announced in advance and will only apply from the following renewal, never retroactively. If you disagree, cancel before renewing.

7.Payment, cancellation and refunds

All payment is processed by your device's store (Google Play or the App Store), under that store's terms. We do not receive and do not keep your card details.

Cancel whenever you like, with no penalty and no need to justify: Google Play → Menu → Subscriptions → Prazzo → Cancel (Android), or Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Prazzo → Cancel Subscription (iPhone). The app also has a button that takes you straight there.

When you cancel, you keep Pro until the end of the period already paid for. After that you return to the free plan.

Refunds follow the policy of your device's store, which is who processes the charge. You also keep any statutory withdrawal or refund right available to you under the consumer law of your country.

8.Cancelling does NOT delete your data

If you cancel, if a charge fails, or if for any reason you return to the free plan:

  • You keep seeing all of your data: every sale, every customer, every payment book.
  • You keep editing what already exists.
  • You keep recording payments received.
  • Nothing is deleted.

9.Offline use and subscription checks

Prazzo works without an internet connection: you can register, sell and record payments offline, and everything uploads on its own once the connection returns.

But Prazzo Pro needs to confirm your subscription periodically. If the app goes more than 2 days without managing to reach the internet, the Pro features are paused until the next connection.

This does not cancel your subscription and does not delete anything. As soon as there is internet, Pro comes back on its own, with no new charge and nothing for you to do.

We are stating this prominently because it is a real limitation of the product, and you have the right to know before subscribing.
